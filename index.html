Since 2015 I am a member of the Flow Analysis and Simulation Team of the University of West Attica, which offers research and consulting services in Fluid Flow, Hydraulics and Convective Heat Transfer, including Magnetohydrodynamics, Turbomachinery, Bioengineering, Thermal Analysis, Turbulence, Multiphase Flow, Industrial and Environmental Applications and Fluid-Structure Interaction.
